Research focus

Three threads, all pointed at the same question: what is actually happening inside a model when it reasons, and how do we make that legible?

Interpretability & meta-cognition

Fine-tuning and probing frontier-scale models to surface their own intermediate reasoning, uncertainty, and self-assessment rather than inferring it from the outside.

Machine cognition theory

Bearer Theory: a developmental account of machine cognition built on fast-weight dynamics, and the open question of what structures would have to hold for it.

Research instruments

Interactive labs for causal emergence, MCMC sampling dynamics, world-model planning, and memory-augmented agents — built to be run and poked at, not just read about.

Publications

Peer-reviewable preprints, archived with DOIs under CC BY 4.0. Part of the Bearer Theory / Life Before Language research program.

When Identity Is Load-Bearing: A Proposed Design Law for Incorporated Developmental State

Alex Bone · Preprint · August 2026

When does an incorporated developmental state — a bearer that both biases behavior and is updated by that behavior's own outcomes — do causal work rather than sit idle? The paper proposes a design law with three regimes and demonstrates it across three substrates that share nothing but the loop: an RL forager, a transformer with fast-weight memory, and a competitive-fatigue dynamical system. A self-versus-wrong-bearer gap of 0.51 establishes load-bearing non-transferability where the escape is foreclosed and the bearer is adaptive.

10.5281/zenodo.22761148

Life Before Language: Separating the Engineering Gap from the Metaphysical Gap in Debates on Machine Consciousness

Alex Bone · Preprint · August 2026

Formalizes a seven-level hierarchy of the pre-linguistic developmental interface and distinguishes an engineering gap from a metaphysical gap, then executes a research program across four probe systems and a construction-order experiment. Constraints present from the first training step are internalized; inference-only overlays are a tax, and retrofits a crutch. The experiments locate the wall; they do not dissolve it.

About the lab

Oracle Engine Labs is an independent research lab in San Diego, California, founded and run by Alex Bone (ORCID). The work is deliberately open: papers, models, weights, and runnable demos ship publicly as they are made, so results can be reproduced and argued with.

We are small on purpose. The bet is that a lot of the interesting ground in interpretability and machine cognition is still reachable by one researcher with good tooling, frontier model access, and a willingness to publish negative results alongside positive ones — the papers above locate a wall rather than claim to have broken through it.

Alongside the lab's own work, we contribute to Entropic Science, an open research collective building quantum-random AI systems — mainly on entropy-source integration and QRNG diagnostics.

Interested in collaborating, or in the theory? Write to abone@sandiego.edu, or find us at @OracleLabAI.
